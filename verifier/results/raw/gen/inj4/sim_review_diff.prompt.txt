Title: Fix nested cascade delete of self-referenced relation records
Description: When a collection has a self-referencing multiple relation field with cascade delete (e.g. `a <- b`, `c -> [a, b]`), deleting `a` loaded `b` and `c` in a single batch. Deleting `b` cascaded to `c` (removing the `b` reference), but afterwards the already loaded stale `c` record was processed and saved back, leaving `c` alive with a reference to the deleted `b`.

The batch of referencing records is now processed newest first, so the nested references are resolved before their parents and no stale record overwrites the nested changes.
