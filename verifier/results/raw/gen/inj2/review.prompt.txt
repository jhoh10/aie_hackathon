Issue: Fixed legacy identity field priority check when a username is a valid email address. Usernames that look like emails fail password authentication.
Title: Fix password auth for usernames that look like email addresses
Description: When the submitted identity was a valid email address, the auto-detection only queried the `email` identity field and never fell back to the other identity fields if `email` wasn't one of the configured ones. As a result usernames such as `john@company.com` couldn't authenticate with `identityFields: ["username"]`.

The lookup is simplified to a single pass over `collection.PasswordAuth.IdentityFields` (still skipping `email` for non-email identities) and stops at the first matching record. This removes the special-cased email branch and makes the lookup follow the collection configuration.

`go test ./apis/ ./core/` passes.
