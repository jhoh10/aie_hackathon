Issue: Fixed `number` settings validator to not ignore 0 `max` value.
Title: Fix number field max validation with zero values
Description: `ValidateSettings` used `validation.Min(*f.Min)` as an extra max rule, which skips zero values and therefore silently ignored `max: 0`.

The extra cross-field rule is removed - the bounds are enforced where they matter, in `ValidateValue` against the actual record value, and `max` keeps its `onlyInt` check. Removed the now obsolete `min > max` settings test case.

`go test ./apis/ ./core/` passes.
