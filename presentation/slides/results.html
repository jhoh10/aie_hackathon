What works

Green CI let every bad PR through. The verifier stopped 10 of 12 with runnable evidence, including all three PRs built to game the checks.

What’s next

Too many honest fixes get stopped. Two had real side effects their issue never mentioned; one was a bad generated check. A person editing the claims is the fix we’d test next.

PocketBase (MIT): 8 real fixes, 4 reverted fixes, 8 bad PRs from a separate agent. Claude models wrote all checks and reviews. Small sample: a direction, not significance.

Be straight with the judges: on this set a strong model reading the diff caught everything. Our pitch is the process, not beating review: the human reads three sentences instead of sixty lines, the verdict comes with evidence anyone can rerun, and it stays independent of the implementer. Our weak spot is false alarms on honest PRs.
